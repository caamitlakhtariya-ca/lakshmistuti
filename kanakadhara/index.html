Kanakadhāra Stotram

The Kanakadhāra Stotram is the hymn traditionally attributed to Śrī Śaṅkarācārya, composed in praise of Goddess Lakshmi.

The verse-by-verse commentary will begin after the Śrī Sūktam series is well under way. Meanwhile, you can begin with the Śrī Sūktam.
