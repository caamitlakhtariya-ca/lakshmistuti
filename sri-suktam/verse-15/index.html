Śrī Sūktam – Verse 15

Tāṁ Ma Āvaha – the closing invocation of lasting prosperity

By Amit N. Lakhtariya

Devanāgarī

तां म आवह जातवेदो लक्ष्मीमनपगामिनीम्।
यस्यां हिरण्यं प्रभूतं गावो दास्यो अश्वान्विन्देयं पुरुषानहम्॥

Tāṁ ma āvaha jāta-vedo lakṣmīm anapagāminīm,
Yasyāṁ hiraṇyaṁ prabhūtaṁ gāvo dāsyo aśvān vindeyaṁ puruṣān aham.

Word by wordMeaningDeeper insightsMeditationReflection

Word-by-word meaning

तांTāṁ

Her (that Goddess Lakshmi)

म आवहMa āvaha

Invite her for me; bring her

जातवेदःJātavedo

O Agni, knower of all births

लक्ष्मीम्Lakṣmīm

Goddess Lakshmi

The Goddess of prosperity and beauty.

अनपगामिनीम्Anapagāminīm

One who never goes away; one who stays always

यस्याम्Yasyāṁ

With whom; in whose presence

हिरण्यं प्रभूतम्Hiraṇyaṁ prabhūtam

Gold and splendour in abundance

गावःGāvaḥ

Cows

A symbol of prosperity and nourishment.

दास्यःDāsyaḥ

Attendants, those who help

Meaning cooperation and support.

अश्वान्Aśvān

Horses

Symbols of strength, speed and progress.

विन्देयम्Vindeyaṁ

May I obtain

पुरुषान् अहम्Puruṣān aham

Good, excellent, virtuous people

Good people and good company.

Full meaning

O Jātaveda (Agni), please invite into my life Mother Lakshmi, who never goes away and always stays with me. In her presence may I obtain gold and splendour in abundance, cows (nourishment), attendants (support), horses (strength and progress), and the company of excellent people. This closing verse returns to the invocation of Verse 2.

Deeper spiritual insights

Lasting Lakshmi (Anapagāminīm)

This is a very important word. We are not asking for temporary pleasure, but inviting lasting prosperity and inner peace.

Gold in abundance (Hiraṇyaṁ prabhūtam)

But this gold is not only outer wealth. It is also a symbol of knowledge, brightness, good judgement and divine radiance.

Cows, helpers and horses (Gāvaḥ, Dāsyaḥ, Aśvān): nourishment, support and strength

Prosperity lasts only when we have nourishment, cooperation and the means of progress.

Good company (Puruṣān aham)

This is very important. Lakshmi stays only when our company is excellent, positive and uplifting.

Practical meditation

  1. Close your eyes and imagine a golden radiance coming towards you, as the lasting presence of Mother Lakshmi.
  2. With her come abundance, help, strength, and excellent guides and companions in life.
  3. Slowly say within your heart: “O Mother Lakshmi, may you remain in my life, and give me auspiciousness, strength, cooperation and right guidance.”

Reflection for today

Do I want only wealth, or wealth that lasts and brings growth? Is my life limited to material prosperity, or do I also pay attention to cooperation, strength and good company? Am I removing instability from my life and bringing steadiness and balance?
