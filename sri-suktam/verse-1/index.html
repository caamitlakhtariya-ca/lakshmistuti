Śrī Sūktam – Verse 1

Hiraṇyavarṇāṁ Hariṇīṁ – the invitation to Goddess Lakshmi

By Amit N. Lakhtariya

Devanāgarī

हिरण्यवर्णां हरिणीं सुवर्णरजतस्रजाम्।
चन्द्रां हिरण्मयीं लक्ष्मीं जातवेदो म आवह॥

Hiraṇyavarṇāṁ hariṇīṁ suvarṇa-rajata-srajām,
Chandrāṁ hiraṇmayīṁ Lakṣmīṁ jāta-vedo ma āvaha.

Word by wordMeaningDeeper insightsMeditationReflection

Word-by-word meaning

हिरण्यवर्णांHiraṇyavarṇāṁ

Golden-colored one

The golden glow stands for spiritual illumination, purity and auspiciousness.

हरिणींHariṇīṁ

Resembling a deer

Gentle and graceful: it points to gentleness, beauty, purity and swiftness.

सुवर्णरजतस्रजाम्Suvarṇa-rajata-srajām

Wearing garlands (adornments) of gold and silver

Suvarṇa = gold, rajata = silver, srajām = garlanded or adorned. It speaks of richness, prosperity, brilliance, divine radiance and cosmic abundance.

चन्द्रांChandrāṁ

Radiant like the moon

Coolness, soothing grace, serenity, beauty and balanced emotions.

हिरण्मयीं लक्ष्मींHiraṇmayīṁ Lakṣmīṁ

The golden form of Goddess Lakshmi

Divine abundance, prosperity and spiritual richness.

जातवेदो म आवहJātavedo ma āvaha

O Jātaveda (Fire, Agni), please invite or bring her to me

Jātaveda means “one who knows all births”. Agni is the mediator who carries our prayers to the Divine.

Full meaning

O Jātaveda (sacred fire, knower of all births), I humbly request you to invite into my life the golden-hued, graceful, gentle Goddess Lakshmi, who is adorned with garlands of gold and silver, who radiates serenity like the soft glow of the moon. May she gracefully enter my life, bestowing purity, peace, prosperity and spiritual harmony.

Deeper spiritual insights

Gentleness (Hariṇīṁ)

True abundance and fulfilment are drawn not through harsh pursuit but through gentleness, grace and purity of heart.

The gold and silver garland

Balanced material and spiritual prosperity: gold stands for worldly success, silver for emotional and spiritual wisdom.

Moon-like radiance (Chandrāṁ)

Real prosperity is serene, calm and balanced. It cools the mind, relieves stress and brings peace.

The role of Agni (Jātaveda)

You are not alone on the journey. You ask the divine forces, with Agni as messenger, to guide you and connect you with universal abundance.

Practical meditation

  1. After chanting, visualise Goddess Lakshmi vividly.
  2. Imagine her radiant form, golden like sunrise, her eyes full of compassionate gentleness, like those of a deer.
  3. Feel the serenity of moonlight radiating from her presence into your heart.
  4. Allow the image to settle gently, gradually replacing anxieties or fears with calmness and confidence.

Reflection for today

Through the day, quietly remind yourself of gentleness, grace, clarity and balance, the qualities symbolised by Lakshmi. Notice how these qualities subtly influence your interactions, choices and responses.

This first verse isn’t merely a chant. It is an invitation to the Divine, an invitation to nurture a gentle yet radiant heart that attracts true prosperity effortlessly. May your journey today become meaningful, peaceful and enriching.
