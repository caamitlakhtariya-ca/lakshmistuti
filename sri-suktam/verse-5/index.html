Śrī Sūktam – Verse 5

Chandrāṁ Prabhāsāṁ – surrender, and the choice of Lakshmi

By Amit N. Lakhtariya

Devanāgarī

चन्द्रां प्रभासां यशसा ज्वलन्तीं श्रियं लोके देवजुष्टामुदाराम्।
तां पद्मिनीमीं शरणमहं प्रपद्येऽलक्ष्मीर्मे नश्यतां त्वां वृणे॥

Chandrāṁ prabhāsāṁ yaśasā jvalantīṁ śriyaṁ loke devajuṣṭām udārām,
Tāṁ padminīm īṁ śaraṇam ahaṁ prapadye alakṣmīr me naśyatāṁ tvāṁ vṛṇe.

Word by wordMeaningDeeper insightsMeditationReflection

Word-by-word meaning

चन्द्राम्Chandrāṁ

Shining and gladdening, moon-like

Symbolises coolness, beauty, purity, emotional calm and grace.

प्रभासाम्Prabhāsāṁ

Full of brilliant light

Refers to divine brightness, an inner spiritual glow.

यशसा ज्वलन्तीम्Yaśasā jvalantīm

Glowing with fame, success and honour

True prosperity brings not just wealth, but respect and renown earned through good deeds.

श्रियं लोके देवजुष्टाम्Śriyaṁ loke devajuṣṭām

That Śrī (Lakshmi), adored in the world and favoured by the gods themselves

उदाराम्Udārām

Generous, magnanimous

True abundance is never stingy; it is generous, open-hearted and sharing.

तां पद्मिनीम्Tāṁ padminīm

That lotus-bearing Goddess

The small word īṁ that follows is a Vedic particle of emphasis, usually left untranslated.

शरणमहं प्रपद्येŚaraṇam ahaṁ prapadye

I take refuge in her; I surrender myself at her feet

This is the deepest gesture of trust and humility, acknowledging that divine grace guides prosperity.

अलक्ष्मीर्मे नश्यताम्Alakṣmīr me naśyatām

May all forms of misfortune (Alakṣmī) disappear from my life

Alakṣmī is the opposite of Lakshmi: poverty, scarcity, misfortune and negativity.

त्वां वृणेTvāṁ vṛṇe

I choose you (O Lakshmi); I seek you alone

A conscious choice to align with prosperity, grace and righteousness, rejecting all that is inauspicious.

Full meaning

I surrender myself to that radiant, moon-like, luminous Goddess Lakshmi, who shines with fame, grace and generosity, adored by the gods and revered by all. I take refuge in this lotus-bearing Goddess. May all misfortune vanish from my life; O Divine Lakshmi, I choose you and your grace alone.

Deeper spiritual insights

Choosing prosperity consciously (Tvāṁ vṛṇe)

The verse teaches us that prosperity and misfortune are both energies we choose to invite or reject. Reflection: am I consciously choosing prosperity in thought, word and action every day?

The removal of Alakṣmī (misfortune)

Prosperity is not just about attracting good; it is about cleansing negativity: scarcity mindsets, bad habits, laziness or unethical actions that block abundance. Reflection: what “Alakṣmī” habits or energies do I need to consciously let go of today?

Magnanimity and generosity (Udārām)

True wealth radiates and uplifts others. Generosity amplifies prosperity. Reflection: where can I be more giving and open-hearted in my life?

Surrender (Śaraṇam ahaṁ prapadye)

We do our part, but then we surrender the outcome to divine grace. Prosperity flows best when action meets surrender.

Practical meditation

  1. Close your eyes and visualise the radiant form of Goddess Lakshmi, soft as moonlight yet brilliantly glowing.
  2. See her holding a lotus, smiling with compassion and generosity.
  3. Whisper softly in your heart: “O Divine Mother, I surrender at your feet. May all misfortune dissolve. May I embody light, grace, generosity and abundance. I choose you and your path.”

Reflection for today

What negative patterns (Alakṣmī) am I willing to consciously let go of? Where can I express more generosity (Udārām)? How can I trust and surrender more, while still taking inspired action?

This verse is a powerful turning point. You are not just calling prosperity; you are declaring your conscious alignment with it, and rejecting scarcity, fear and negativity.
