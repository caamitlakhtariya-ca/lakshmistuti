The verse

॥ ऋचा ९ ॥

गन्धद्वारां दुराधर्षां नित्यपुष्टां करीषिणीम्।
ईश्वरीं सर्वभूतानां तामिहोपह्वये श्रियम्॥

॥ श्री महालक्ष्म्यै नमः ॥

Gandha-dvārāṁ durādharṣāṁ nitya-puṣṭāṁ karīṣiṇīm,
Īśvarīṁ sarva-bhūtānāṁ tām ihopahvaye śriyam.

Meaning in short

I invoke here and now that Goddess Śrī, whose presence is known by divine fragrance, who is invincible, ever-nourishing and ever-growing. She is the sovereign queen, protector of all beings, and the source of continuous abundance and fruitfulness.

Quiet as fragrance, unshakeable, ever nourished and sovereign: the Goddess as a steady, protective presence.

Key highlights

  • Gandha-dvārā Fragrance: a quiet presence, sensed not announced
  • Durādharṣā Unshaken: grace that challenges cannot overcome
  • Nitya-puṣṭā Nourished: steady, not peaks and valleys
  • Īśvarī Sovereign: protector of all beings

Explore the four qualities

Gandha-dvārāFragrance

Gandha-dvārā: her presence is known by fragrance, as through a door. The commentary notes it is not loud or ostentatious but sensed subtly, like scent in the air.

Pause and reflect: What quiet presence am I leaving through my actions and words?

DurādharṣāUnshaken

Durādharṣā means one who cannot be overcome. The commentary reads it as wealth and grace that challenges cannot shake, rooted in more than outside conditions.

Pause and reflect: Does my sense of having enough depend on how things look today?

Nitya-puṣṭāNourished

Nitya-puṣṭā is ever nourished and ever flourishing. The commentary says prosperity should not be a game of peaks and valleys, but a steady nurturing of resources, relationships and health.

Pause and reflect: What small habit could keep my health and relationships nourished?

ĪśvarīSovereign

Īśvarī sarva-bhūtānām: sovereign of all beings. The commentary reads her as protector of everyone, so prosperity that is good for all is the kind said to be blessed and sustained.

Pause and reflect: Is what I am pursuing good for others as well as for me?

Notes on each word
गन्धद्वाराम्Gandha-dvārām

One who is fragrant and subtle. This represents subtle presence, purity and auspiciousness. Wherever Lakshmi resides, there is a feeling of freshness and sacredness, like divine perfume.

दुराधर्षाम्Durādharṣām

True abundance and divine grace are unshakeable by external negativity.

नित्यपुष्टाम्Nitya-puṣṭām

Real prosperity is continuous, self-renewing and never depleting.

करीषिणीम्Karīṣiṇīm

Especially connected with fertile land and cattle manure, symbols of nourishment in Vedic culture. It represents fruitfulness, growth and productivity.

ईश्वरीम्Īśvarīm

This line reminds us that Lakshmi is not just gentle and beautiful; she is supreme authority and power.

सर्वभूतानाम्Sarva-bhūtānām

Belonging to and protecting all living creatures.

श्रियम्Śriyam

The embodiment of prosperity and grace.

Deeper spiritual insights

Interpretation and reflection: ideas that help us read the verse, not part of the text itself.

Fragrance (Gandha-dvārām)

Lakshmi’s presence is not loud or ostentatious. It is sensed subtly, like fragrance in the air. Reflection: am I cultivating a quiet, fragrant presence in my actions and thoughts?

Invincibility (Durādharṣām)

True wealth and grace cannot be shaken by challenges. Reflection: is my sense of abundance strong and rooted, or dependent on external conditions?

Continuous nourishment (Nitya-puṣṭām)

Prosperity should not be a peak-and-valley game. Reflection: am I nurturing my resources, relationships and health consistently?

Fruitfulness (Karīṣiṇīm)

True abundance is not stagnant. It grows, multiplies and benefits others.

Universal protector (Sarva-bhūtānām Īśvarīm)

The Goddess protects all. Prosperity that is good for everyone is blessed and sustained.

Practical meditation

  1. Imagine a soft, divine fragrance filling your space.
  2. See Goddess Lakshmi seated on a radiant throne, unshakeable, powerful, gently smiling.
  3. Around her is flourishing greenery, fruits and flowing rivers, symbols of endless growth and productivity.
  4. Whisper softly within: “I invite you, O invincible, ever-nourishing, sovereign Goddess. May your presence fill my life with peace, fragrance, abundance and protection.”

Reflection for today

How can I make my prosperity “fragrant”: subtle, graceful and uplifting to those around me? Where in my life do I need to cultivate consistency (nitya-puṣṭām) rather than sporadic effort? Am I making my wealth and blessings fruitful for others too?

This verse teaches that true prosperity is fragrant, unshakeable, ever-growing and protective. It is not flashy but felt quietly, a presence that nourishes all who come near.
