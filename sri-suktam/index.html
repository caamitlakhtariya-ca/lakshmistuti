Śrī Sūktam – verse by verse

The Śrī Sūktam is a Vedic hymn praising Goddess Lakshmi (Śrī) as the source of prosperity, grace and auspiciousness. Each verse here has the Sanskrit text, a word-by-word meaning, deeper insights and a short practice.

Verses are published one at a time, so that each can be read and absorbed slowly before the next appears.

A note on the count: the Śrī Sūktam is usually counted as 15 verses. A few closing verses on the fruits of reciting it (the phalaśruti) are chanted after them, and some editions number those too, so you may see a slightly higher total elsewhere.
