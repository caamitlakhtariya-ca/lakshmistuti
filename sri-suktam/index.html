Śrī Sūktam – verse by verse

The Śrī Sūktam (also written Sri Suktam, Shri Suktam or Shree Suktam) is a Vedic hymn praising Goddess Lakshmi (Śrī) as the source of prosperity, grace and auspiciousness. Each verse here has the Sanskrit text, a word-by-word meaning, deeper insights and a short practice.

Verses are published one at a time, so that each can be read and absorbed slowly before the next appears.

A note on the count: the hymn proper has 15 verses. Here the closing verse on the fruit of reciting it (the phalaśruti) is numbered Verse 16, so the series is complete at sixteen. Editions differ: some print more phalaśruti verses, and some do not number this one at all.

Verse 1

Hiraṇyavarṇāṁ Hariṇīṁ – the invitation to Goddess Lakshmi

Verse 2

Tāṁ Ma Āvaha – the Goddess who never leaves

Verse 3

Aśvapūrvāṁ Rathamadhyāṁ – the grand arrival of Lakshmi

Verse 4

Kāṁ Sosmitāṁ – Lakshmi, golden-walled, compassionate and radiant

Verse 5

Chandrāṁ Prabhāsāṁ – surrender, and the choice of Lakshmi

Verse 6

Ādityavarṇe – the sun-like Goddess, tapas and the bilva tree

Verse 7

Upaitu Māṁ Devasakhaḥ – honour and prosperity in society

Verse 8

Kṣut-pipāsā-malāṁ – clearing scarcity and negativity from home and heart

Verse 9

Gandha-dvārāṁ – the fragrant, invincible, ever-nourishing Goddess

Verse 10

Manasaḥ Kāmam – pure desires, truthful speech, enduring honour

Verse 11

Kardamena Prajābhūtā – Lakshmi as the nurturing mother

Verse 12

Āpaḥ Sṛjantu – softness, sacred waters and a settled presence

Verse 13

Ārdrāṁ Puṣkariṇīṁ – tender, nourishing, golden

Verse 14

Ārdrāṁ Yaḥ Kariṇīṁ – tender, active and sun-bright

Verse 15

Tāṁ Ma Āvaha – the closing invocation of lasting prosperity

Verse 16

Yaḥ Śuciḥ Prayato Bhūtvā – the phalaśruti, the fruit of reciting the hymn
