Vedic hymn to Goddess Lakshmi

Śrī Sūktam – verse by verse

The Śrī Sūktam (also written Sri Suktam, Shri Suktam or Shree Suktam) is a Vedic hymn praising Goddess Lakshmi (Śrī) as the source of prosperity, grace and auspiciousness. Each verse here has the Sanskrit text, a word-by-word meaning, deeper insights and a short practice.

The verses

1Verse 1Hiraṇyavarṇāṁ Hariṇīṁ – the invitation to Goddess Lakshmi2Verse 2Tāṁ Ma Āvaha – the Goddess who never leaves3Verse 3Aśvapūrvāṁ Rathamadhyāṁ – the grand arrival of Lakshmi4Verse 4Kāṁ Sosmitāṁ – Lakshmi, golden-walled, compassionate and radiant5Verse 5Chandrāṁ Prabhāsāṁ – surrender, and the choice of Lakshmi6Verse 6Ādityavarṇe – the sun-like Goddess, tapas and the bilva tree7Verse 7Upaitu Māṁ Devasakhaḥ – honour and prosperity in society8Verse 8Kṣut-pipāsā-malāṁ – clearing scarcity and negativity from home and heart9Verse 9Gandha-dvārāṁ – the fragrant, invincible, ever-nourishing Goddess10Verse 10Manasaḥ Kāmam – pure desires, truthful speech, enduring honour11Verse 11Kardamena Prajābhūtā – Lakshmi as the nurturing mother12Verse 12Āpaḥ Sṛjantu – softness, sacred waters and a settled presence13Verse 13Ārdrāṁ Puṣkariṇīṁ – tender, nourishing, golden14Verse 14Ārdrāṁ Yaḥ Kariṇīṁ – tender, active and sun-bright15Verse 15Tāṁ Ma Āvaha – the closing invocation of lasting prosperity16Verse 16Yaḥ Śuciḥ Prayato Bhūtvā – the phalaśruti, the fruit of reciting the hymn

Verses are published one at a time, so that each can be read and absorbed slowly before the next appears. A note on the count: the hymn proper has 15 verses. Here the closing verse on the fruit of reciting it (the phalaśruti) is numbered Verse 16, so the series is complete at sixteen. Editions differ: some print more phalaśruti verses, and some do not number this one at all.
