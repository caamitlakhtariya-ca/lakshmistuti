Śrī Sūktam – Verse 13

Ārdrāṁ Puṣkariṇīṁ – tender, nourishing, golden

By Amit N. Lakhtariya

Devanāgarī

आर्द्रां पुष्करिणीं पुष्टिं पिङ्गलां पद्ममालिनीम्।
चन्द्रां हिरण्मयीं लक्ष्मीं जातवेदो म आवह॥

Ārdrāṁ puṣkariṇīṁ puṣṭiṁ piṅgalāṁ padma-mālinīm,
Chandrāṁ hiraṇmayīṁ lakṣmīṁ jāta-vedo ma āvaha.

Word by wordMeaningDeeper insightsMeditationReflection

Word-by-word meaning

आर्द्राम्Ārdrām

Moist, tender, compassionate, nurturing

Lakshmi’s presence is soft and nourishing, never dry or harsh.

पुष्करिणीम्Puṣkariṇīṁ

One who dwells in lotus-filled lakes

A source of purity and beauty.

पुष्टिम्Puṣṭiṁ

Fullness, nourishment, flourishing health and prosperity

पिङ्गलाम्Piṅgalām

Golden-hued, shining with warmth and radiance

पद्ममालिनीम्Padma-mālinīm

Adorned with a garland of lotuses

Symbolising divine beauty and purity.

चन्द्राम्Chandrāṁ

Shining and gladdening, moon-like

Radiant and soothing like the moon.

हिरण्मयीम्Hiraṇmayīṁ

Golden, lustrous, and filled with divine glow

लक्ष्मीम्Lakṣmīṁ

Goddess of prosperity, beauty, grace and abundance

जातवेदो म आवहJātavedo ma āvaha

O Agni (Jātaveda, divine fire), please bring her to me

Invite this divine presence into my life.

Full meaning

O Jātaveda (sacred fire), please bring into my life that tender, nurturing, lotus-dwelling Goddess Lakshmi, who is full of nourishment, golden-hued, adorned with lotuses, radiant like the moon, and glowing with divine brilliance.

Deeper spiritual insights

Ārdrām: softness and compassion

Prosperity is beautiful only when it is tender, caring and filled with empathy.

Puṣkariṇīṁ: a lake filled with lotuses

It symbolises purity, serenity and peace. True abundance is as peaceful and beautiful as a still lake filled with flowers.

Puṣṭiṁ: nourishment

Without nourishment of body, mind and soul, wealth is hollow.

Piṅgalām and Hiraṇmayīṁ: golden glow

Prosperity should make life warm, bright and inviting, like the warm glow of gold or the soft light of the moon.

Agni as the messenger (Jātaveda)

We once again ask the sacred fire, the purifier and messenger, to invite this divine presence into our lives.

Practical meditation

  1. Close your eyes and visualise a serene lake, covered in soft pink lotuses, glowing under moonlight.
  2. See Goddess Lakshmi rising from this lake: golden, radiant, wearing a lotus garland, her eyes filled with compassion and warmth.
  3. Whisper inwardly: “May this tenderness, nourishment and brilliance flow into my home and heart, and dwell here always.”

Reflection for today

Am I cultivating softness and grace along with strength and ambition? Does my abundance radiate beauty and nourish others? Can I bring more calm, peaceful energy into my home and work, like the stillness of a lotus lake?

This verse teaches that prosperity is not only power. It is gentle, radiant, nourishing beauty that brings peace and fulfilment.
