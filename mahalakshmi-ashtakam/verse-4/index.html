Mahālakṣmī Aṣṭakam – Verse 4

Siddhi, buddhi, bhukti, mukti – success and wisdom, enjoyment and freedom

By Amit N. Lakhtariya

Devanāgarī

सिद्धिबुद्धिप्रदे देवि भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायिनि।
मन्त्रमूर्ते सदा देवि महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते॥

Siddhi-buddhi-prade devi bhukti-mukti-pradāyini,
Mantra-mūrte sadā devi mahālakṣmi namo 'stu te.

Word by wordMeaningDeeper insightsMeditationReflection

Word-by-word meaning

सिद्धिबुद्धिप्रदेSiddhi-buddhi-prade

O giver of accomplishment and wisdom

Siddhi is accomplishment or success; buddhi is intellect, discernment; prada is giver.

देविDevi

O Goddess

Vocative.

भुक्तिमुक्तिप्रदायिनिBhukti-mukti-pradāyini

O giver of worldly enjoyment and liberation

Bhukti is enjoyment of the world, material well-being; mukti is liberation; pradāyinī is giver.

मन्त्रमूर्तेMantra-mūrte

O you whose form is mantra

Mūrti is form or embodiment. The Goddess is seen as present in sacred sound.

सदाSadā

Always

At all times.

महालक्ष्मिMahālakṣmi

O Mahalakshmi

The refrain.

Full meaning

Salutations to you, O Goddess, giver of accomplishment and wisdom, giver of worldly well-being and of liberation, whose very form is mantra. O Mahalakshmi, I bow to you.

Deeper spiritual insights

In simple words

Two pairs of gifts are named: success with wisdom, and enjoyment with liberation. Notice that success is paired with wisdom, and enjoyment with freedom. The verse does not set one pair against the other.

Both aims of life

Indian thought speaks of bhukti, a good life in the world, and mukti, freedom from what binds us. Here the Goddess gives both. It is a quietly balanced view: material well-being is not despised, and it is not the final goal.

Mantra-mūrti: sound as presence

To call the Goddess “whose form is mantra” is to say that when her name or verse is recited, she is present in the sound. This is the devotional logic behind chanting, and it explains why many readers value pronouncing the verses with care.

Practical meditation

  1. Minute 1: settle your posture and breath.
  2. Minute 2: recite the verse once, slowly and clearly.
  3. Minute 3: think of one area where you want success and ask what wisdom it needs.
  4. Minute 4: sit quietly with the sound of the verse still in your mind.

Reflection for today

Where am I asking for success without asking for wisdom? What would a good life, not only a successful one, look like for me?

The verse pairs every gift with its companion. That is perhaps its best advice: look for success that comes with wisdom, and enjoyment that does not forget freedom. This meditation is a devotional practice for attention and reflection; it does not promise a supernatural result or guarantee material wealth.
