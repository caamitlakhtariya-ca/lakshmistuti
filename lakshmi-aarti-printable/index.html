Lakshmi Aarti at home

A one-page guide to keep beside your aarti booklet. It does not print the words of the aarti, so sing from the version your family or temple uses.

Keep ready

  • Image or idol of the Goddess on a clean cloth
  • A thali with a ghee lamp, incense, flowers and kumkum
  • Camphor or a second small lamp for the aarti
  • A small bell, if you have one
  • Sweets or fruit for the offering (prasad)
  • Your aarti booklet or printed words

The steps

  1. Finish the puja first. Offer flowers, kumkum and sweets before the aarti begins.
  2. Light the lamp. Hold the thali in your right hand and keep the left free for the bell.
  3. Wave the light. Move the thali slowly in a clockwise circle before the image while the hymn is sung. Follow your family’s number of turns.
  4. Take the aarti. When the hymn ends, pass your hands over the flame and touch them to your eyes and forehead.
  5. Offer the prasad and stay a minute in quiet gratitude.

Safety. Keep dupattas, long sleeves and curtains away from the flame, and keep children back from the lit thali.

What each stanza says

  • Refrain. “Victory to Mother Lakshmi”: a thank-you for food, work, family and health.
  • 1st stanza. Lakshmi beside Vishnu: wealth should walk with right conduct.
  • 2nd stanza. One Mother, many names: the goddesses are faces of one divine feminine.
  • 3rd stanza. The stainless Durgā: prosperity needs courage and steady character.
  • 4th stanza. From the unseen to the seen: effort and grace work together.
  • 5th stanza. Where she dwells, good qualities come: virtues, not gold, make a home.
  • 6th stanza. Nothing happens without her: behind every meal stand many hands, so give back.
  • 7th stanza. Born of the milk ocean: the source of the jewels of the churning.
  • Final stanza. The fruit of singing: a glad heart and the strength to turn from our faults.

A closing prayer

O Mother Mahalakshmi, give us enough wealth to live with dignity and to meet our duties, and the wisdom never to let the pursuit of wealth make us forget our dharma. Give us courage in hard times and humility when success comes. Bless our homes with food, peace and understanding. Make us worthy keepers of the prosperity we ask for.
